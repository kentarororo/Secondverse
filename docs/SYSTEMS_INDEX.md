This index is intentionally lean until the combat laboratory direction is approved.

| Order | System | Player-facing proof | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Battle presentation | A player reads actor, target, action, impact, status, and advantage from the battlefield | Taste gate pending |
| 2 | Deterministic combat | Same seed and commands reproduce identical typed events and results | Planned |
| 3 | Formation and policy | A major preparation change produces a traceably different battle | Planned |
| 4 | Authored heroes and encounters | Three distinct heroes face enemies with visible, counterable rules | Planned |
| 5 | Battle explanation | The decisive moment is understandable without a full text log | Planned |
| 6 | Character consequence | A battle produces one persistent development fact and follow-up decision | Planned |

## Quarantined scope

Procedural quests, a six-chapter compiler, prose-heavy campaign flow, crafting, multiple currencies, world-review screens, meta progression, and broad content expansion are outside the prototype.
