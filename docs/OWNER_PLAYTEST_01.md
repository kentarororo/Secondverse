# Owner Playtest 01 — First Encounter Pair

**Time:** 5–8 minutes  
**Build:** local combat laboratory on `codex/reimagine-core-loop`  
**Purpose:** decide whether the first pair is worth revising or extending. This is not a general polish review.

## Open the build

From the repository root:

```powershell
npm run playtest
```

Open the URL printed by Vite: `http://127.0.0.1:4173/Secondverse/`.

The published build uses the same project path: `https://kentarororo.github.io/Secondverse/`.

For a clean save, use a new private/incognito window. If you reuse a normal window, clear site data for `127.0.0.1:4173` before starting.

## Play without reading this section ahead

1. Keep playback at **1×**. Read the Pressure the Rear tell, then choose any formation, team policy, and hero policy that make sense to you.
2. Start the battle. Keep the event inspector closed unless the battlefield becomes unclear.
3. At the result, say aloud why you think the fight changed or ended. Choose one item for a stated reason, confirm it, and continue.
4. On Punish the Front preparation, say what the item and any condition will change. Play the encounter.
5. Return to Pressure the Rear and make one different plan. Stop after deciding whether you actually want to watch that replay.

## Record immediately

- Was the battle enjoyable to watch at normal speed? **Yes / Mixed / No**
- Without the inspector, who acted, who was targeted, what happened, and what changed the fight?
- What plan did you change because of the enemy tell?
- Which item did you choose, and what did you expect it to do?
- Did a hero become Bruised, or did you receive No injury? What caused that result?
- Before step 5 asked for a replay, had you already wanted another attempt? **Yes / No**
- Name one moment that worked and one moment that felt flat or unclear.
- Strict recommendation: **KEEP / REVISE / REMOVE**

## Gate rule

Do not add encounters three and four from this build alone. Record the answers in `production/PLAYTEST_LOG.md`; then either revise the failed moment or define the next encounter-pair question.

## Known limitations

- The trio is authored; generated-hero attachment is a later, separate gate.
- Visuals and audio are representative temporary assets, not final art direction.
- Only two encounters and one aftermath condition exist.
- Formation and policy choices reset with the page; confirmed equipment and the aftermath condition persist.
